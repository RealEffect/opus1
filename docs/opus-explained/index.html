01编码端Encoder

采样：把声波变成数字Sampling: turning sound waves into numbers

编码器拿到的不是声波，而是一串整数。先看这串整数是怎么来的。The encoder does not receive a sound wave; it receives a sequence of integers. First, let’s see where those integers come from.

声音是空气压强的快速起伏。麦克风把压强变成连续变化的电压。模数转换器（ADC，analog-to-digital converter）每隔固定的时间读一次电压，这叫采样；再把读数取整到有限个等级之一，这叫量化。得到的整数序列叫 PCM（脉冲编码调制，pulse-code modulation）信号。Sound is a rapid fluctuation in air pressure. A microphone turns the pressure into a continuously varying voltage. An analog-to-digital converter (ADC) reads the voltage at fixed time intervals; this is called sampling. It then rounds each reading to one of a finite number of levels; this is called quantization. The resulting sequence of integers is called a pulse-code modulation (PCM) signal.

采样率决定能记录的最高频率。按奈奎斯特–香农采样定理，采样率为 fs 时，只有低于 fs/2 的频率能被准确还原。高于它的频率会变成一个较低的“假”频率，这种现象叫混叠。所以 ADC 之前总有一个低通滤波器，先把过高的频率滤掉。The sample rate sets the highest frequency that can be recorded. By the Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem, at a sample rate of fs, only frequencies below fs/2 can be reconstructed accurately. A frequency above that limit turns into a lower, “false” frequency. This is called aliasing. That is why an ADC is always preceded by a low-pass filter, which removes the frequencies that are too high.

位深决定每个读数的精度。取整带来的误差叫量化噪声。每多 1 位，量化噪声大约低 6 dB。The bit depth sets the precision of each reading. The error introduced by rounding is called quantization noise. Each extra bit lowers the quantization noise by about 6 dB.

Opus 接受 8、12、16、24、48 kHz 五种采样率的 PCM 输入，每个样本是 16 位整数或浮点数（libopus 1.6 还新增了 24 位整数接口）。44.1 kHz 这类其他采样率要先由应用自己重采样。Opus accepts PCM input at five sample rates: 8, 12, 16, 24, and 48 kHz. Each sample is a 16-bit integer or a floating-point number (libopus 1.6 also added a 24-bit integer interface). Other sample rates, such as 44.1 kHz, must first be resampled by the application.

不压缩的 PCM 很占空间。48 kHz、16 位的单声道信号每秒有 48 000 × 16 = 768 000 比特，也就是 768 kb/s。用 Opus 以 32 kb/s 编码同样的信号，数据量只有原来的 1/24。下面几节讲这 24 倍是从哪里省出来的。Uncompressed PCM takes a lot of space. A 48 kHz, 16-bit mono signal carries 48 000 × 16 = 768 000 bits per second, or 768 kb/s. Encoded with Opus at 32 kb/s, the same signal takes only 1/24 as much data. The next few sections explain where this factor of 24 comes from.

深入一点Going deeper 量化噪声的 6 dB 规则The 6 dB rule for quantization noise

用 b 位均匀量化一个满幅正弦波，量化误差近似为均匀分布的白噪声，信噪比约为：When a full-scale sine wave is quantized uniformly with b bits, the quantization error is approximately uniformly distributed white noise, and the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is about:

SNR ≈ 6.02 · b + 1.76 dB16 位约 98 dB，8 位约 50 dBabout 98 dB at 16 bits, about 50 dB at 8 bits

这个噪声在 0 到 fs/2 之间平均分布。后面会看到，Opus 的思路刚好相反：它并不追求每个样本的误差都小，而是控制噪声出现在哪些频率、有多大，让耳朵听不出来。This noise is spread evenly from 0 to fs/2. As later sections show, Opus takes the opposite approach. It does not try to keep the error small on every sample. Instead, it controls at which frequencies the noise appears and how loud it is there, so that the ear cannot hear it.

02编码端Encoder

压缩从哪里来Where the compression comes from

Opus 能省掉九成以上的数据，靠的是三件事。Opus 内部有两套编码器：擅长语音的 SILK 和适合任意声音的 CELT（第 4 节细讲）。第 5、6、7 节分别讲这三件事的具体做法。Opus can remove more than 90% of the data, and it does so with three ideas. Inside, Opus has two encoders: SILK, which is good at speech, and CELT, which suits any sound (Section 4 covers both in detail). Sections 5, 6, and 7 show how each of the three ideas is put into practice.

SILK 用它（第 5 节）Used by SILK (Section 5)

1. 信号是可以预测的1. The signal is predictable

语音的下一个样本，大部分能由前面十几个样本的加权和算出来。浊音还会按基音周期重复。只编码“预测不中的部分”，要传的数据就少得多。The next speech sample can largely be computed as a weighted sum of the dozen or so samples before it. Voiced sounds also repeat with the pitch period. Encoding only “the part the prediction misses” leaves far less data to send.

CELT 用它（第 6 节）Used by CELT (Section 6)

2. 耳朵分辨不了所有细节2. The ear cannot resolve every detail

耳朵把声音按频带来感知。响亮的声音会盖住同一频带里较弱的声音，这叫掩蔽。所以在每个频带里，耳朵主要在意总能量对不对，对能量在带内如何分布不太敏感。耳朵在高频处分得很粗，一个频带可以宽到几千赫兹（CELT 最高的频带有 4400 Hz 宽），一个能量值就能代表这么宽的一段频谱。于是可以先把每个频带的能量编准，再用少量比特粗略描述细节，并让量化噪声落在响亮的成分附近，被它们掩蔽。The ear perceives sound in frequency bands. A loud sound hides weaker sounds in the same band; this is called masking. So within each band, the ear mainly cares whether the total energy is right, and it is not very sensitive to how that energy is distributed within the band. At high frequencies the ear’s resolution is coarse, so a band can be several thousand hertz wide (CELT’s highest band is 4400 Hz wide), and a single energy value can stand for that whole stretch of spectrum. The encoder can therefore code each band’s energy accurately first, then describe the details roughly with a few bits, and place the quantization noise near loud components, where they mask it.

两层共用（第 7 节）Used by both layers (Section 7)

3. 常见的值用更短的编码3. Common values get shorter codes

预测误差、能量变化这些数值，小值出现得多，大值出现得少。区间编码按概率分配长度：常见的值只花零点几比特，罕见的值才花更多比特。For values such as prediction errors and energy changes, small values occur often and large values rarely. Range coding assigns code lengths by probability: a common value costs only a fraction of a bit, and only rare values cost more bits.

有损：解码出的波形和原来不同Lossy: the decoded waveform differs from the original

丢掉信息的是量化，也就是把数值取整到有限的精度。SILK 把预测误差取整成小整数；CELT 把每个频带的能量和频带内部的形状都只编到有限的精度，比特越少，形状描述得越粗。码率低时，编码器还会直接不编码高频。预测和区间编码本身都能完全还原，不丢信息。解码后的波形和原始波形逐点比较并不一样，但设计目标是让人耳听不出区别，或者区别尽量小。第 10 节可以直接对比。Information is lost in quantization, that is, in rounding values to a limited precision. SILK rounds the prediction error to small integers. CELT codes both the energy of each band and the shape within the band to a limited precision; the fewer the bits, the more coarsely the shape is described. At low bitrates, the encoder also leaves out the high frequencies altogether. Prediction and range coding are themselves fully reversible and lose no information. Compared sample by sample, the decoded waveform is not the same as the original, but the design goal is that the ear cannot hear the difference, or that the difference is as small as possible. Section 10 lets you compare them directly.

03编码端Encoder

分帧：每 20 毫秒一个数据包Framing: one packet every 20 ms

实时通话不能等一句话说完再压缩。编码器把信号切成等长的小段，一段一段地处理。A real-time call cannot wait until a whole sentence has been spoken before compressing it. The encoder cuts the signal into short pieces of equal length and processes them one at a time.

每一小段叫一帧。编码器每收到一帧 PCM，就输出一个数据包。帧越长，编码效率越高：频率分辨率更细，每帧固定要传的参数也摊得更薄。但帧越长，延迟也越大，因为必须等整帧到齐才能开始编码。Each piece is called a frame. For every frame of PCM it receives, the encoder outputs one packet. Longer frames code more efficiently: the frequency resolution is finer, and the parameters that must be sent with every frame are spread over more audio. But longer frames also add more delay, because encoding cannot start until the whole frame has arrived.

Opus 的帧长可以是 2.5、5、10、20、40 或 60 毫秒，默认 20 毫秒（40、60 毫秒时，一个包里可能装着几个 20 毫秒的帧，见第 8 节）。48 kHz 下，20 毫秒就是 960 个样本。An Opus frame can be 2.5, 5, 10, 20, 40, or 60 ms long, and the default is 20 ms (at 40 and 60 ms, one packet may hold several 20 ms frames; see Section 8). At 48 kHz, 20 ms is 960 samples.

除了等一整帧，编码器还要多看后面一小段信号，这叫前瞻（look-ahead）。默认前瞻是 6.5 毫秒，所以 20 毫秒一帧时，算法延迟是 20 + 6.5 = 26.5 毫秒（图 16 有完整的延迟预算）。Besides waiting for a whole frame, the encoder also needs to see a short stretch of the signal that follows it. This is called lookahead. The default lookahead is 6.5 ms, so with 20 ms frames the algorithmic delay is 20 + 6.5 = 26.5 ms (Figure 16 shows the full delay budget).

码率有三种控制方式。可变码率（VBR，variable bitrate）让难编的帧多用字节、安静的帧少用字节。受约束的可变码率（constrained VBR）也让每帧大小不同，但编码器会记下累计多用或少用了多少字节，并据此限制后面的帧，所以平均码率始终贴近目标。恒定码率（CBR，constant bitrate）让每个包一样大，适合带宽固定的信道。libopus 默认用受约束的可变码率，但约束只在只用 CELT 的模式里起作用；opus1 也照此处理。There are three ways to control the bitrate. Variable bitrate (VBR) gives more bytes to frames that are hard to code and fewer to quiet frames. Constrained VBR also lets the frame size vary, but the encoder keeps a running count of how many bytes it has used above or below the target and limits later frames accordingly, so the average bitrate always stays close to the target. Constant bitrate (CBR) makes every packet the same size, which suits channels with a fixed bandwidth. libopus uses constrained VBR by default, but the constraint only takes effect in CELT-only mode; opus1 does the same.

深入一点Going deeper 帧长和延迟的取舍Frame length versus delay

帧长直接计入延迟。在一帧之外，编码器还要往后多看一小段信号，叫前瞻（look-ahead）。CELT 的窗口和下一帧重叠 2.5 毫秒，所以它需要 2.5 毫秒前瞻。SILK 需要更多：5 毫秒用于噪声整形分析，最多 1.5 毫秒用于重采样。为了让两层对齐，编码器把 CELT 的输入也推迟 4 毫秒。总前瞻因此是 2.5 + 4 = 6.5 毫秒，默认配置的算法延迟是 20 + 6.5 = 26.5 毫秒。这是 libopus 的默认值，opus1 实测相同（48 kHz 下延迟 312 个样本）。图 16 有完整的延迟预算。The frame length adds directly to the delay. Beyond the frame itself, the encoder also looks a short stretch further into the signal; this is the lookahead. CELT’s window overlaps the next frame by 2.5 ms, so CELT needs 2.5 ms of lookahead. SILK needs more: 5 ms for noise shaping analysis and up to 1.5 ms for resampling. To keep the two layers aligned, the encoder also delays the CELT input by 4 ms. The total lookahead is therefore 2.5 + 4 = 6.5 ms, and the algorithmic delay in the default configuration is 20 + 6.5 = 26.5 ms. This is the libopus default, and measurements of opus1 give the same value (a delay of 312 samples at 48 kHz). Figure 16 shows the full delay budget.

40 和 60 毫秒的包用于更看重效率的场景。SILK 可以把 40、60 毫秒当作一个 Opus 帧来编，内部是 2 或 3 个 20 毫秒的 SILK 帧；CELT 一帧最长 20 毫秒，更长的包由多个 20 毫秒的帧拼成。Packets of 40 and 60 ms are for uses where efficiency matters more. SILK can code 40 or 60 ms as a single Opus frame, made up internally of 2 or 3 SILK frames of 20 ms. A CELT frame is at most 20 ms long; longer packets are built from several 20 ms frames.

04编码端Encoder

选模式：SILK、CELT 与混合Choosing a mode: SILK, CELT, and hybrid

Opus 里其实有两个编码器。每个包都要先决定用哪一个，或者两个一起用。Opus actually contains two encoders. For every packet, it first has to decide which one to use, or whether to use both.

SILK · 来自 SkypeSILK · from Skype

线性预测语音编码器Linear predictive speech coder

内部采样率 8、12 或 16 kHz，最高覆盖 8 kHz。低码率下编语音非常省，但不适合音乐。Its internal sample rate is 8, 12, or 16 kHz, and it covers frequencies up to 8 kHz. It codes speech very efficiently at low bitrates, but it is not suited to music.

CELT · 来自 Xiph.OrgCELT · from Xiph.Org

变换编码器Transform coder

全称 Constrained Energy Lapped Transform，意思是“约束能量的重叠变换”。内部总是 48 kHz，最高覆盖 20 kHz，适合音乐和任意声音，帧可以短到 2.5 毫秒。The name stands for Constrained Energy Lapped Transform. It always runs internally at 48 kHz and covers frequencies up to 20 kHz. It suits music and any other sound, and its frames can be as short as 2.5 ms.

由此有三种模式：This gives three modes:

  • SILK-only 只用 SILK。用于低码率语音，音频带宽为窄带、中带或宽带。SILK-only Uses only SILK, for low-bitrate speech with narrowband, mediumband, or wideband audio bandwidth.
  • 混合模式（Hybrid） 8 kHz 以下交给 SILK，8 kHz 以上交给 CELT，两者写进同一个包。用于中等码率、需要超宽带或全带的语音。Hybrid SILK codes everything below 8 kHz and CELT everything above 8 kHz, and both are written into the same packet. Used for medium-bitrate speech that needs super-wideband or fullband audio bandwidth.
  • CELT-only 只用 CELT。用于音乐、高码率和最低延迟。CELT-only Uses only CELT, for music, high bitrates, and the lowest delay.

音频带宽指一个包编码到的最高频率。Opus 有五档：窄带（NB）4 kHz、中带（MB）6 kHz、宽带（WB）8 kHz、超宽带（SWB）12 kHz、全带（FB）20 kHz。注意全带是 20 kHz，不是 48 kHz 采样率对应的 24 kHz：Opus 从不编码 20 kHz 以上的声音。Audio bandwidth is the highest frequency that a packet codes. Opus has five levels: narrowband (NB) 4 kHz, mediumband (MB) 6 kHz, wideband (WB) 8 kHz, super-wideband (SWB) 12 kHz, and fullband (FB) 20 kHz. Note that fullband is 20 kHz, not the 24 kHz that a 48 kHz sample rate allows: Opus never codes sound above 20 kHz.

编码器为每个包重新选择模式。依据是目标码率、应用类型和帧长。应用类型有五种：语音通话 Voip、一般音频 Audio、只用 CELT 的受限低延迟 RestrictedLowDelay，以及 libopus 1.6 新增的 RestrictedSilk 和 RestrictedCelt。后两种分别只用 SILK 或只用 CELT，给解码器只有其中一层的接收端用。libopus 和 opus1 还会按码率调整带宽，码率低时主动降到宽带甚至窄带。复杂度较高时（见本节的“深入一点”），它们还会检测输入实际有多宽的频带，并据此降低带宽。The encoder chooses the mode anew for every packet. The choice depends on the target bitrate, the application, and the frame length. There are five applications: Voip for voice calls, Audio for general audio, RestrictedLowDelay (a restricted low-delay setting that uses only CELT), and two that are new in libopus 1.6, RestrictedSilk and RestrictedCelt. These two use only SILK or only CELT, for a receiver whose decoder has only that layer. libopus and opus1 also adjust the bandwidth to the bitrate; at low bitrates they deliberately drop to wideband or even narrowband. At higher complexity settings (see “Going deeper” in this section), they also detect how wide the input’s actual bandwidth is and lower the bandwidth to match.

深入一点Going deeper 模式切换Mode switching

两层的解码状态互相不能用。码流在 CELT-only 与 SILK-only 或混合模式之间切换时，编码器会在包里多带一个 5 毫秒的 CELT “冗余帧”，解码器用它做交叉淡化，让切换处不出现咔嗒声。libopus 和 opus1 的编码器都写冗余帧，两者的解码器也都用它。冗余帧放不下时（例如码率很低），编码器就不带它。这时解码器用前一种模式的丢包隐藏补出一段过渡音频：开头 2.5 毫秒直接用它，接下来的 2.5 毫秒再和新帧交叉淡化。The decoder state of one layer is of no use to the other. When the stream switches between CELT-only and SILK-only or hybrid mode, the encoder adds an extra 5 ms CELT “redundant frame” to the packet. The decoder uses it to cross-fade, so that no click is heard at the switch. The encoders of libopus and opus1 both write redundant frames, and both decoders use them. When there is no room for a redundant frame (for example, at a very low bitrate), the encoder leaves it out. The decoder then makes transition audio with the packet loss concealment of the previous mode: the first 2.5 ms are this audio, and the next 2.5 ms cross-fade it with the new frame.

libopus 在复杂度较高时（浮点版本 7 及以上，定点版本 10）还会分析输入是语音还是音乐、实际有多宽的频带，并据此调整模式和带宽。opus1 移植了浮点版本的这部分分析（Cargo 特性 analysis，默认开启），也从复杂度 7 起运行。分析不运行时（复杂度低于 7、输入是 8 或 12 kHz，或者 opus1 编译时关掉了这个特性），两者都按应用类型使用固定的估计：Voip 偏向语音，Audio 偏向音乐。At higher complexity settings (7 and above in the floating-point build, 10 in the fixed-point build), libopus also analyzes whether the input is speech or music and how wide its actual bandwidth is, and adjusts the mode and bandwidth accordingly. opus1 ports the floating-point version of this analysis (the Cargo feature analysis, on by default) and also runs it from complexity 7. When the analysis does not run (below complexity 7, with 8 or 12 kHz input, or in an opus1 build without that feature), both use a fixed estimate based on the application: Voip leans toward speech, and Audio toward music.

RFC 6716 给出了 20 毫秒帧下各种用途的推荐码率：窄带语音 8–12 kb/s，宽带语音 16–20 kb/s，全带语音 28–40 kb/s，全带单声道音乐 48–64 kb/s，全带立体声音乐 64–128 kb/s。RFC 6716 recommends bitrates for various uses with 20 ms frames: narrowband speech 8–12 kb/s, wideband speech 16–20 kb/s, fullband speech 28–40 kb/s, fullband mono music 48–64 kb/s, and fullband stereo music 64–128 kb/s.

05编码端EncoderSILK

SILK：用预测压缩语音SILK: compressing speech with prediction

SILK 不直接编码波形，而是编码“一个发声模型的参数”，再加上模型预测不中的误差。SILK does not encode the waveform directly. Instead, it encodes “the parameters of a model of speech production,” plus the error that the model fails to predict.

人发声的过程可以近似成两部分。声源：发浊音（如元音）时声带周期性振动，产生一串间隔相等的气流脉冲；发清音（如 s、f）时气流穿过狭窄处，产生类似噪声的气流。滤波器：口腔和鼻腔像一个可调的共鸣腔，在频谱上加出几个峰，叫共振峰。不同的元音对应不同的共振峰位置。这叫源–滤波器模型。Human speech production can be roughly modeled as two parts. The source: in voiced sounds (such as vowels), the vocal folds vibrate periodically and produce a train of evenly spaced pulses of air. In unvoiced sounds (such as s and f), air passes through a narrow constriction and becomes a noise-like flow. The filter: the mouth and nasal cavities act like an adjustable resonator that adds several peaks to the spectrum, called formants. Each vowel has its own formant positions. This is called the source–filter model.

SILK 把这个过程反过来做：SILK runs this process in reverse:

  1. 短时预测（LPC，linear predictive coding，线性预测编码）：用前 16 个样本（窄带和中带用 10 个）的加权和预测当前样本。这组权重描述的就是声道滤波器，也就是频谱的包络。把预测误差写成 e[n] = x[n] − Σ ak·x[n−k]，从 x 算出 e 的这个滤波器记作 A(z)。反过来，用 e 合成 x 的滤波器写作 1/A(z)，它的频率响应就是频谱包络。Short-term prediction (linear predictive coding, LPC): the current sample is predicted as a weighted sum of the previous 16 samples (10 in narrowband and mediumband). These weights describe the vocal-tract filter, that is, the spectral envelope. The prediction error is e[n] = x[n] − Σ ak·x[n−k], and the filter that computes e from x is denoted A(z). Conversely, the filter that synthesizes x from e is written 1/A(z), and its frequency response is the spectral envelope.
  2. 长时预测（LTP，long-term prediction）：浊音每隔一个基音周期就近似重复一次，再用一个周期之前的波形预测当前波形。Long-term prediction (LTP): voiced speech roughly repeats itself once every pitch period, so SILK then predicts the current waveform from the waveform one period earlier.
  3. 两次预测之后剩下的误差叫激励。这个名字来自源–滤波器模型里的声源，但两者不完全一样：长时预测已经去掉了浊音的周期性，所以 SILK 的激励里看不出基音脉冲。它的幅度比原信号小得多，频谱接近平坦。把长时预测加回去，才接近图 5 里的那串周期脉冲（图 6 最下一行就是这样画的）。SILK 把激励量化成整数“脉冲”，和模型参数一起编码。激励的每个样本都要编码，所以它占的比特反而最多：在图 6 的 16 kb/s 码流里，约占每帧比特的 3/4。The error left after both predictions is called the excitation. The name comes from the source in the source–filter model, but the two are not quite the same. Long-term prediction has already removed the periodicity of voiced speech, so no pitch pulses are visible in SILK's excitation. Its amplitude is much smaller than that of the original signal, and its spectrum is nearly flat. Only when the long-term prediction is added back does it resemble the periodic pulse train in Figure 5 (this is how the bottom row of Figure 6 is drawn). SILK quantizes the excitation into integer “pulses” and encodes them together with the model parameters. Every sample of the excitation has to be encoded, so, perhaps surprisingly, the excitation takes the most bits: in the 16 kb/s bitstream of Figure 6, about 3/4 of the bits in each frame.

SILK 的一帧是 20 毫秒或 10 毫秒，默认 20 毫秒。20 毫秒的帧分成 4 个 5 毫秒的子帧，10 毫秒的帧分成 2 个。每帧要传的东西大致是：A SILK frame is 20 ms or 10 ms long; the default is 20 ms. A 20 ms frame is divided into four 5 ms subframes, and a 10 ms frame into two. Roughly, each frame carries the following:

  • 信号类型：无语音、清音或浊音。编码器先做语音活动检测，判断这一帧有没有人在说话；“无语音”帧里仍可能有背景噪声，不一定是静音。Signal type: inactive, unvoiced, or voiced. The encoder first runs voice activity detection (VAD) to decide whether anyone is speaking in this frame. An “inactive” frame may still contain background noise; it is not necessarily silent.
  • LPC 系数：每帧一组，先换算成 NLSF（归一化线谱频率，normalized line spectral frequencies）再量化。NLSF 是同一组信息的另一种表示，更容易量化，也容易保证合成滤波器稳定（输出不会越来越大、失控）。20 毫秒的帧还带一个插值系数：码流里的这组 NLSF 描述后 10 毫秒，前 10 毫秒可以用上一帧和这一帧之间的插值。LPC coefficients: one set per frame, converted to normalized line spectral frequencies (NLSFs) before quantization. NLSFs are another representation of the same information. They are easier to quantize, and they make it easy to keep the synthesis filter stable (so that its output does not keep growing out of control). A 20 ms frame also carries an interpolation factor: the NLSFs in the bitstream describe the last 10 ms, and the first 10 ms can use values interpolated between the previous frame and this one.
  • 增益：每个子帧一个，表示激励的大小。Gains: one per subframe, giving the level of the excitation.
  • 基音周期和 LTP 滤波器：只有浊音帧才有，每个子帧一组 5 个系数的滤波器（5 抽头）。Pitch period and LTP filter: present only in voiced frames. Each subframe has one filter with 5 coefficients (5 taps).
  • 激励脉冲：每个样本一个小整数，大多是 0 或 ±1。Excitation pulses: one small integer per sample, mostly 0 or ±1.
深入一点Going deeper 噪声整形与量化Noise shaping and quantization

激励量化必然带来误差。SILK 的噪声整形量化器（NSQ，noise shaping quantizer）在量化时加了反馈，让量化噪声的频谱跟着语音的包络走：共振峰处的噪声多一些，被响亮的语音掩蔽；共振峰之间的噪声少一些。这样做并不会减少总噪声能量：码率相同时，整形后的总噪声能量通常比不整形时更大。换来的好处是，噪声集中在响亮的语音能盖住的频率上，所以更难被听到。噪声整形只发生在编码器里，整形用的滤波器不传给解码器。所以以后改进听觉模型，不需要改码流格式。Quantizing the excitation always introduces error. SILK's noise shaping quantizer (NSQ) adds feedback during quantization so that the spectrum of the quantization noise follows the envelope of the speech. There is more noise at the formants, where the loud speech masks it, and less noise between the formants. This does not reduce the total noise energy: at the same bitrate, the total noise energy after shaping is usually higher than without shaping. The benefit is that the noise is concentrated at frequencies that the loud speech can cover, so it is harder to hear. Noise shaping happens only in the encoder, and the shaping filters are not sent to the decoder. So the perceptual model can be improved later without changing the bitstream format.

预测方程写成：The prediction equation is:

e[n] = x[n] − Σk=1..16 ak · x[n−k]LPC 分析：求出使 e 的能量最小的 akLPC analysis: find the ak that minimize the energy of e

LPC 系数由 Burg 方法求得。浊音帧先做长时预测，LPC 是在长时预测之后的残差上估计的。基音周期的搜索范围是 2 到 18 毫秒（对应约 55 到 500 Hz），分三级：先在 4 kHz 的降采样信号上粗搜，再在 8 kHz 和原采样率上细化。增益在对数域量化成 64 级，每级约 1.37 dB。The LPC coefficients are computed with the Burg method. In voiced frames, long-term prediction is done first, and the LPC coefficients are estimated on the residual left after long-term prediction. The pitch period is searched over 2 to 18 ms (about 55 to 500 Hz) in three stages: a coarse search on the signal downsampled to 4 kHz, then refinement at 8 kHz and at the original sample rate. The gains are quantized in the log domain to 64 levels, about 1.37 dB apart.

激励脉冲按 16 个样本一组，用“壳编码”（shell coding）编码：先编码这一组脉冲的总数，再递归地编码前一半和后一半各占多少，直到单个样本。The excitation pulses are coded in blocks of 16 samples with shell coding. First the total number of pulses in the block is coded. Then, recursively, the number of pulses in each half is coded, down to single samples.

06编码端EncoderCELT

CELT：变换、能量与形状CELT: transform, energy, and shape

CELT 把每帧信号变到频率域，按频带切开，每个频带分成“有多响”和“长什么样”两部分分别编码。CELT transforms each frame into the frequency domain and splits it into bands. Each band is coded in two separate parts: “how loud it is” and “what it looks like.”

CELT 对每一帧做这几步（图 7 把它们拆开，可以逐步查看）：CELT takes each frame through the following steps (Figure 7 separates them so that you can go through them one at a time):

  1. 加窗和变换：加窗之前，CELT 先做预加重 y[n] = x[n] − 0.85·x[n−1]，压低低频、略抬高高频。然后用 MDCT（modified discrete cosine transform，改进离散余弦变换）把 20 毫秒的信号变成 960 个频率系数，每个系数间隔 25 Hz，其中 20 kHz 以下的 800 个会被编码。MDCT 和傅里叶变换类似，每个系数表示这一帧里某个频率的余弦分量有多强；不同的是，它的系数都是实数。每次变换读入 22.5 毫秒（1080 个样本），两端各有 2.5 毫秒和相邻帧重叠。窗口每次往后移 960 个样本，换来 960 个系数，所以数据量没有增加。读入的样本比系数多，单独一帧做逆变换还原不出原信号，要和相邻帧在重叠区相加才行（见图 13）。CELT 的窗口只重叠 2.5 毫秒，中间是平的。重叠越短，编码器要往后多等的时间越少，这是 CELT 延迟低的原因。Windowing and transform: before windowing, CELT applies pre-emphasis, y[n] = x[n] − 0.85·x[n−1], which lowers the low frequencies and slightly raises the high ones. It then uses the modified discrete cosine transform (MDCT) to turn 20 ms of signal into 960 frequency coefficients, spaced 25 Hz apart. The 800 coefficients below 20 kHz are coded. The MDCT is similar to the Fourier transform: each coefficient says how strong the cosine component at one frequency is in this frame. Unlike the Fourier transform, its coefficients are all real numbers. Each transform reads 22.5 ms (1080 samples), and 2.5 ms at each end overlaps the neighboring frame. The window moves forward by 960 samples each time and yields 960 coefficients, so the amount of data does not grow. Because the transform reads more samples than it produces coefficients, the inverse transform of a single frame cannot restore the original signal. The result has to be added to the neighboring frames in the overlap regions (see Figure 13). CELT's window overlaps by only 2.5 ms and is flat in the middle. The shorter the overlap, the less time the encoder has to wait for later samples, and this is why CELT's delay is low.
  2. 分频带：把系数分成 21 个频带，低频窄、高频宽（见图 2）。Splitting into bands: the coefficients are grouped into 21 bands, narrow at low frequencies and wide at high frequencies (see Figure 2).
  3. 编码能量：先编码每个频带的能量。这里的“能量”指带内系数平方和的平方根 √(Σx²)，实际上是幅度；libopus 的代码里也把它叫作能量（energy）。即使比特很少，频谱的大致轮廓也是对的。“约束能量”指的就是这一步：不论形状编得多粗，解码后每个频带的能量都等于码流里传过去的值。能量分两次编码。第一次以 6 dB 为一级编一个粗略值，叫粗能量；它先用上一帧同一频带和本帧前面的频带做预测，只编码预测误差，所以通常只花几个比特。第二次在分配比特之后，用分到的比特把它编得更细，叫精细能量。Coding the energy: the energy of each band is coded first. “Energy” here means the square root of the sum of the squared coefficients in the band, √(Σx²), which is really an amplitude; the libopus code also calls it energy. Even with very few bits, the overall outline of the spectrum is right. The “constrained energy” in CELT's name refers to this step: however coarsely the shape is coded, each band's decoded energy equals the value sent in the bitstream. The energy is coded in two passes. The first pass codes a rough value in 6 dB steps, called the coarse energy. The coarse energy is first predicted from the same band in the previous frame and from the preceding bands in this frame, and only the prediction error is coded, so it usually costs only a few bits. The second pass comes after bit allocation. It uses the bits allocated to the band to code the energy more precisely; this is called the fine energy.
  4. 编码形状：每个频带的系数除以这个 √(Σx²)，剩下一个长度为 1 的向量，表示能量在频带内如何分布。用 PVQ（金字塔向量量化，见下文）编码这个向量。Coding the shape: the coefficients of each band are divided by this √(Σx²). What remains is a vector of length 1 that describes how the energy is distributed within the band. This vector is coded with pyramid vector quantization (PVQ, see below).

形状怎么编码：PVQHow the shape is coded: PVQ

PVQ（pyramid vector quantization，金字塔向量量化）用 K 个“单位脉冲”去逼近形状向量。每个脉冲放在某一维上，可正可负，同一维可以放多个。所有可能的摆法组成一个码本：N 维、K 个脉冲时，所有整数向量满足 |y1| + … + |yN| = K。这里 N 是频带里的系数个数，例如 20 毫秒帧里最低的频带有 8 个系数，就是 8 维。码本里的每个点叫一个码字。这些点在空间里排成一个“金字塔”（高维的正八面体）的表面，PVQ 因此得名。把选中的整数向量缩放到长度 1，就是解码出的形状。Pyramid vector quantization (PVQ) approximates the shape vector with K “unit pulses.” Each pulse goes in one dimension, with either sign, and one dimension can hold several pulses. All possible placements form a codebook: with N dimensions and K pulses, it is the set of all integer vectors with |y1| + … + |yN| = K. Here N is the number of coefficients in the band. For example, the lowest band of a 20 ms frame has 8 coefficients, so it is 8-dimensional. Each point in the codebook is called a codeword. These points lie on the surface of a “pyramid” (the higher-dimensional version of a regular octahedron), which gives PVQ its name. Scaling the chosen integer vector to length 1 gives the decoded shape.

K 越大，码本越密，形状越准，要的比特也越多。码本里的点数可以精确算出来，所以编码器只需传一个序号，用 log2(码本大小) 比特。The larger K is, the denser the codebook, the more accurate the shape, and the more bits it needs. The number of points in the codebook can be computed exactly, so the encoder only has to send an index, which takes log2(codebook size) bits.

比特怎么分给各个频带How the bits are divided among the bands

一帧的比特预算是固定的。CELT 内置一张分配表，有 11 行，每行规定每个频带里“每个系数”分多少比特，从第 0 行的全 0，到第 10 行的最多。编码器和解码器用同样的算法，在相邻两行之间插值，找到刚好用完预算的那一组分配。分配结果本身不传输：解码器自己就能算出同样的结果，编码器只传几个小的调整量，比如整体向低频或高频倾斜多少、哪几个频带额外加一点（叫动态分配，dynalloc，图 9 里用三角标出）。The bit budget of a frame is fixed. CELT has a built-in allocation table with 11 rows. Each row specifies how many bits “per coefficient” each band gets, from all zeros in row 0 up to the most in row 10. The encoder and the decoder run the same algorithm: they interpolate between two adjacent rows to find the allocation that uses up the budget exactly. The allocation itself is not transmitted, because the decoder can compute the same result on its own. The encoder sends only a few small adjustments, such as how far the whole allocation tilts toward low or high frequencies, and which bands get a little extra (called dynamic allocation, or dynalloc, and marked with triangles in Figure 9).

粗能量和帧开头的几个标志（比如是不是瞬态帧、预滤波器参数）先写进码流，用掉的比特从预算里扣掉，图 9 里叫“头部、粗能量等”。剩下的比特才按分配表分给各频带。The coarse energy and a few flags at the start of the frame (such as whether the frame is a transient frame, and the pre-filter parameters) are written to the bitstream first, and the bits they use are subtracted from the budget. Figure 9 labels them “Header, coarse energy, etc.” Only the remaining bits are divided among the bands according to the allocation table.

每个频带分到的比特，一小部分用来把能量编得更细，其余决定它能用多少个 PVQ 脉冲。比特少时，高频频带可能一个脉冲也分不到。这时解码器不会让它空着，而是把低频频带已经解码出的形状“折叠”（复制）过来，加一点噪声，再按编码好的能量缩放后填进去。这样高频的能量还是对的，听起来不会发闷。Of the bits a band receives, a small part refines its energy, and the rest determine how many PVQ pulses it can use. When bits are scarce, a high band may not get a single pulse. The decoder does not leave such a band empty. Instead, it “folds” (copies) the shape already decoded for the lower bands into it, adds a little noise, and scales the result to the coded energy. The energy at high frequencies is then still correct, so the sound does not become muffled.

瞬态：鼓点前面的“预回声”Transients: the “pre-echo” before a drum hit

变换编码有一个典型问题。量化噪声会均匀散布在整个变换窗口里。如果窗口里有一次突然的敲击，敲击之前原本安静的那一段也会出现噪声，听起来像声音提前响了一点，这叫预回声。CELT 检测到这种瞬态时，把一个 20 毫秒的长变换（长块）换成 8 个短变换（短块），每个只有 120 个系数、每隔 2.5 毫秒一个，噪声就被限制在敲击附近。帧长不变，包也还是一个。Transform coding has a well-known problem. Quantization noise spreads evenly over the whole transform window. If the window contains a sudden hit, noise also appears in the quiet stretch before the hit, so the sound seems to start slightly early. This is called pre-echo. When CELT detects such a transient, it replaces the single 20 ms long transform (a long block) with 8 short transforms (short blocks). Each has only 120 coefficients, and they are spaced 2.5 ms apart. This confines the noise to the neighborhood of the hit. The frame length stays the same, and it is still one packet.

深入一点Going deeper 能量的量化、基音预滤波Energy quantization, pitch pre-filter

频带能量是频带内系数平方和的平方根，在以 2 为底的对数域里编码。1 个单位是 6.02 dB。能量先以 6 dB 为一级编码，叫粗能量。每个频带的能量先做预测，只编码预测误差。时间方向的预测是上一帧同一频带的能量乘一个系数，20 毫秒帧里是 0.5。频率方向的预测是本帧前面各频带已编码的预测误差之和乘一个系数，20 毫秒帧里是 0.8（等于 1 − β，β = 0.2 是 RFC 6716 §4.3.2.1 预测滤波器里的参数）。预测误差用拉普拉斯分布（两边按指数衰减的分布）建模，小值很常见，所以平均花不了几个比特。比特有富余时，再补几位更细的量化，叫精细能量。The band energy is the square root of the sum of the squared coefficients in the band, and it is coded in the base-2 log domain. One unit is 6.02 dB. The energy is first coded in 6 dB steps; this is the coarse energy. The energy of each band is predicted, and only the prediction error is coded. The prediction across time is the energy of the same band in the previous frame, multiplied by a coefficient that is 0.5 in 20 ms frames. The prediction across frequency is the sum of the already-coded prediction errors of the preceding bands in this frame, multiplied by a coefficient that is 0.8 in 20 ms frames (this equals 1 − β, where β = 0.2 is a parameter of the prediction filter in RFC 6716 §4.3.2.1). The prediction error is modeled with a Laplace distribution (a distribution that decays exponentially on both sides). Small values are very common, so on average the error costs only a few bits. When bits are left over, a few more bits of finer quantization are added; this is the fine energy.

对音高稳定的信号，CELT 在 MDCT 之前先用一个梳状滤波器（基音预滤波器）削弱谐波，解码端再用对应的后滤波器把谐波加回来。后滤波器同时也作用在量化噪声上：谐波之间的噪声被压低，谐波处的噪声被响亮的谐波盖住。基音周期的范围是 15 到 1022 个样本（48 kHz 下约 3.2 kHz 到 47 Hz）。For signals with a steady pitch, CELT first attenuates the harmonics with a comb filter (the pitch pre-filter) before the MDCT. The decoder then adds the harmonics back with the matching post-filter. The post-filter also acts on the quantization noise: the noise between the harmonics is suppressed, and the noise at the harmonics is masked by the loud harmonics. The pitch period ranges from 15 to 1022 samples (about 3.2 kHz down to 47 Hz at 48 kHz).

大的频带分到很多比特时，不会直接用很大的 K：编码器把它对半拆开，先编码一个角度 θ，表示能量在两半之间怎么分（cos θ 和 sin θ），再分别编码两半。每个部分的 K 最多 128。另外，脉冲很少时，解码出的形状会集中在少数几个系数上，听起来像口哨。CELT 用一个可逆的旋转把它们稍微“摊开”，叫扩散（spreading）。When a large band receives many bits, it does not use a very large K directly. Instead, the encoder splits the band in half. It first codes an angle θ that says how the energy is divided between the two halves (cos θ and sin θ), and then codes each half separately. Each part has a K of at most 128. Also, when there are very few pulses, the decoded shape is concentrated on a few coefficients and sounds like whistling. CELT applies an invertible rotation that spreads the energy out a little; this is called spreading.

07编码端Encoder

区间编码：把符号变成比特Range coding: turning symbols into bits

前面所有的参数，最后都要变成一串比特。Opus 用区间编码来做这件事，SILK 和 CELT 共用同一个编码器。Every parameter described so far must in the end become a string of bits. Opus does this with range coding, and SILK and CELT share the same range coder.

能量变化、脉冲个数、信号类型，都是取值有限的符号。每种符号都有一张事先定好的概率表，编码器和解码器各存一份。Energy changes, pulse counts, and signal types are all symbols that take one of a limited set of values. Each kind of symbol has a predefined probability table, and the encoder and the decoder each keep a copy of it.

区间编码（range coding，算术编码的一种实现）从一个区间开始。每编码一个符号，就把当前区间按概率表切成几段，只保留这个符号对应的那一段。概率为 p 的符号让区间缩小到原来的 p 倍，相当于花了 −log2p 比特。所有符号编完后，输出几个字节，足以指明最后那个小区间里的一个数。解码器拿到这个数，按同样的概率表一步步反推，就能读出同样的符号。Range coding, an implementation of arithmetic coding, starts from an interval. For each symbol, the coder splits the current interval into pieces according to the probability table and keeps only the piece that belongs to that symbol. A symbol with probability p shrinks the interval to p times its previous width, which costs −log2p bits. After the last symbol, the encoder outputs a few bytes, just enough to identify one number inside the final small interval. The decoder takes this number, retraces the steps with the same probability tables, and reads out the same symbols.

深入一点Going deeper Opus 的区间编码器The Opus range coder

真实实现不用无限精度的小数，而是用 32 位整数保存区间的下界（val）和宽度（rng）。宽度不大于 223 时，就把下界里进位位下面的 8 位（第 30 到 23 位）作为一个字节输出，再把区间放大 256 倍，这叫重新归一化。下界加法可能向前进位，所以输出会延迟一个字节，等进位确定后再写。概率表用 ICDF（inverse cumulative distribution function，反向累积分布）的形式存储，总份数通常是 2 的幂，这样除法可以换成移位。Opus 的概率表都是固定的，不随数据调整，所以编码器和解码器只要按同样的顺序读写，就一定同步。A real implementation does not use fractions of unlimited precision. It keeps the low end of the interval (val) and its width (rng) in 32-bit integers. When the width is 223 or less, the encoder outputs the 8 bits of the low end just below the carry bit (bits 30 to 23) as one byte and then scales the interval up by 256. This is called renormalization. Adding to the low end can produce a carry into earlier bytes, so the output is held back by one byte, and each byte is written only once its carry is settled. Probability tables are stored in inverse cumulative distribution function (ICDF) form. The total count is usually a power of 2, so divisions can be replaced by shifts. All Opus probability tables are fixed and do not adapt to the data, so the encoder and the decoder are always in sync as long as they read and write in the same order.

有些值接近均匀分布，用概率表不会更省，比如 CELT 的精细能量、后滤波器周期的低位和增益、PVQ 序号的低位。这些“原始比特”从包的末尾往前写，和从前往后写的区间编码数据在中间相遇。SILK 不用原始比特。Some values are close to uniformly distributed, so a probability table would not save any bits. Examples are CELT's fine energy, the low bits of the post-filter period, the post-filter gain, and the low bits of PVQ indices. These “raw bits” are written backward from the end of the packet. The range-coded data is written forward from the start, and the two meet somewhere in the middle. SILK does not use raw bits.

08编码端Encoder

数据包：TOC 字节与传输Packets: the TOC byte and transport

一个 Opus 包以一个字节的包头开始，后面是区间编码器输出的字节；包里装多帧时，包头后面还可能有记录帧数或帧字节数的字节（图 12 可以逐种查看）。An Opus packet starts with a one-byte header, followed by the bytes from the range coder. When a packet holds several frames, the header may be followed by bytes that record the number of frames or the size of each frame in bytes (Figure 12 lets you look at each case).

包的第一个字节叫 TOC（table of contents，目录）。它的高 5 位是配置号，从 32 种组合里指出模式、音频带宽和帧长；接着 1 位表示单声道还是立体声；最低 2 位表示包里装了 1 帧、2 帧还是任意多帧。解码器只看这一个字节，就知道该怎么解后面的数据。The first byte of a packet is the TOC (table-of-contents) byte. Its top 5 bits are the configuration number, which picks one of 32 combinations of mode, audio bandwidth, and frame duration. The next bit says whether the audio is mono or stereo. The lowest 2 bits say whether the packet holds 1 frame, 2 frames, or an arbitrary number of frames. From this one byte, the decoder knows how to decode the rest of the data.

一个包什么时候装多帧：CELT 和混合模式的一帧最长 20 毫秒，所以 40 或 60 毫秒的包由 2 或 3 个 20 毫秒的帧拼成；SILK 的 40、60 毫秒算作一个 Opus 帧，内部是 2 或 3 个 20 毫秒的 SILK 帧。同一个包里的帧共用一个 TOC 字节，所以模式、带宽和帧长都相同。When does a packet hold several frames? In CELT-only and hybrid modes a frame is at most 20 ms long, so a 40 or 60 ms packet is made of 2 or 3 frames of 20 ms. In SILK-only mode, 40 or 60 ms counts as a single Opus frame, made of 2 or 3 SILK frames of 20 ms each. The frames in one packet share a single TOC byte, so they all have the same mode, bandwidth, and frame duration.

一帧最多 1275 字节，一个包最多装 120 毫秒的音频。Opus 包本身不记录整个包有多少字节，也没有序号和时间戳。这些由外面的传输层或文件格式负责：实时通话通常放在 RTP（实时传输协议）里，文件通常存成 Ogg Opus（.opus 文件）。TOC 字节，以及多帧包里记录帧数和各帧字节数的那几个字节，都不经过区间编码，所以不用解码也能拆分、拼接数据包。A frame is at most 1275 bytes, and a packet holds at most 120 ms of audio. An Opus packet does not record its own total size in bytes, and it has no sequence number or timestamp. These are left to the transport layer or file format that carries it: real-time calls usually send packets over RTP (Real-time Transport Protocol), and files are usually stored as Ogg Opus (.opus files). The TOC byte is not range coded, and neither are the bytes that record the frame count and frame sizes in a multi-frame packet. So packets can be split and joined without decoding them.

09解码端Decoder

解码：把比特还原成声波Decoding: turning bits back into sound

解码器按和编码器完全相同的顺序读出每个符号，再把编码的每一步倒过来做。The decoder reads every symbol in exactly the same order as the encoder wrote it, and then undoes each step of the encoding.

RFC 6716 只规定了解码器。只要解码器能正确解出，编码器怎么分析、怎么取舍都可以自由改进。这也是 libopus 这些年能不断提升音质、却不改变码流格式的原因。RFC 6716 specifies only the decoder. As long as the decoder can decode the bitstream correctly, the encoder is free to improve how it analyzes the signal and what trade-offs it makes. This is why libopus has kept improving audio quality over the years without changing the bitstream format.

  • SILK 解码：先把整数脉冲还原成激励：加上量化偏移，再按一个伪随机序列翻转正负号（编码器用同一个序列，量化时已经考虑了这一步）。激励乘以增益，经过 LTP 合成滤波器加回周期性，再经过 LPC 合成滤波器 1/A(z) 加回频谱包络，最后重采样到输出采样率。图 6 里的脉冲就是这一步的输入。SILK decoding: first the integer pulses are turned back into the excitation. The decoder adds a quantization offset and then flips signs according to a pseudo-random sequence (the encoder uses the same sequence and already took this step into account when quantizing). The excitation is multiplied by the gain and passed through the LTP synthesis filter, which restores the periodicity. It then goes through the LPC synthesis filter 1/A(z), which restores the spectral envelope. Finally, the result is resampled to the output sample rate. The pulses in Figure 6 are the input to this process.
  • CELT 解码：先解出各频带的能量和形状，没分到脉冲的频带用折叠填充。能量乘形状得到频谱，再做逆 MDCT（IMDCT）变回时间信号，并和上一帧重叠相加。如果编码器开了基音预滤波器，再用后滤波器把谐波加回来。最后做去加重，撤销编码前的预加重。CELT decoding: the decoder first decodes the energy and shape of each band. Bands that received no pulses are filled by folding. Energy times shape gives the spectrum. An inverse MDCT (IMDCT) turns the spectrum back into a time signal, which is overlap-added with the previous frame. If the encoder turned on the pitch pre-filter, the post-filter puts the harmonics back. Finally, de-emphasis undoes the pre-emphasis applied before encoding.
  • 混合模式：两层分别解码，SILK 的输出升采样到 48 kHz，和 CELT 的输出相加。Hybrid mode: the two layers are decoded separately. The SILK output is upsampled to 48 kHz and added to the CELT output.

丢包了怎么办When packets are lost

网络会丢包。一个包没到，解码器也不能停下来，这个包本该包含的那 20 毫秒仍然要照常输出声音。这叫丢包隐藏（PLC，packet loss concealment）：解码器根据上一帧的状态外推。SILK 继续使用上一帧的 LPC 滤波器和基音周期。激励由两部分组成：LTP 滤波器按基音周期重复前面的波形，另外从上一帧的激励里随机抽取样本。每过一个子帧，这两部分都乘以一个小于 1 的系数，所以连续丢包越多，声音越弱。解码器还会叠加舒适噪声（comfort noise）：按之前收到的无语音帧的频谱和增益生成低电平的噪声，所以长时间丢包后听到的是背景噪声，而不是完全无声。只用 CELT 的帧也按基音隐藏：找出前面输出的基音周期，重复最后一个周期，最长 100 毫秒；之后改为生成噪声，各频带的能量逐渐降到背景噪声的水平。opus1 移植了 libopus 1.6.1 的这些做法；在图 14 的实验里，两者补出的样本完全相同。opus1 也移植了 libopus 1.5 起的深度学习丢包隐藏（Deep PLC），但要打开 cargo 特性 deep-plc，并把解码器复杂度设为 5 或更高才会用到；本页的数据没有用它。Networks lose packets. When a packet does not arrive, the decoder cannot stop: it still has to output sound for the 20 ms that the packet should have carried. This is called packet loss concealment (PLC): the decoder extrapolates from the state of the previous frame. SILK keeps using the LPC filter and the pitch period of the previous frame. The excitation has two parts: one is the earlier waveform, repeated at the pitch period by the LTP filter; the other is samples drawn at random from the previous frame's excitation. After each subframe, both parts are multiplied by a factor less than 1, so the more packets are lost in a row, the weaker the sound becomes. The decoder also applies comfort noise generation (CNG): it adds low-level noise built from the spectrum and gain of earlier received frames that had no voice activity. So after a long loss, the listener hears background noise instead of total silence. Lost CELT-only frames are also concealed by repeating the pitch: the decoder finds the pitch period of the earlier output and repeats the last period, for up to 100 ms. After that it generates noise instead, and the energy of each band falls gradually to the level of the background noise. opus1 ports these methods from libopus 1.6.1. In the experiment of Figure 14, the two produce exactly the same concealed samples. opus1 also ports the deep-learning packet loss concealment (Deep PLC) of libopus 1.5 and later. It is used only with the cargo feature deep-plc and a decoder complexity of 5 or more. The data on this page does not use it.

SILK 还可以开启带内前向纠错（FEC，forward error correction；“带内”指冗余数据就写在正常的包里）：在每个包里附带上一帧的一份较粗的副本（LBRR，low bitrate redundancy，低码率冗余）。上一个包丢了，就用下一个包里的副本恢复。opus1 的编码器能写 LBRR，解码器也能用它恢复丢掉的帧（OpusDecoder::decode_fec）。本页的码流都没有开启 FEC，图 14 里的丢包全靠外推。SILK can also turn on in-band forward error correction (FEC). “In-band” means that the redundant data is written inside the normal packets: each packet carries a coarser copy of the previous frame, called low bitrate redundancy (LBRR). If a packet is lost, the copy in the next packet is used to recover it. The opus1 encoder can write LBRR, and the decoder can use it to recover a lost frame (OpusDecoder::decode_fec). None of the streams on this page has FEC turned on, so the losses in Figure 14 are filled in by extrapolation alone.

图中的“波形信噪比”（SNR，signal-to-noise ratio）逐点比较原始波形和解码波形，只能作参考。Opus 追求的是让失真尽量听不出来，而不是让波形逐点一致。码率较高时，拉低这个数字的主要是听不出的差别，而不是编码误差：The “waveform SNR” (signal-to-noise ratio) in the figure compares the original and decoded waveforms sample by sample, so it is only a rough guide. Opus aims to make distortion as inaudible as possible, not to make the waveform match sample by sample. At higher bitrates, what pulls this number down is mostly differences you cannot hear, not coding error:

  • 语音用 Voip 模式，编码器先让输入通过一个高通滤波器，截止频率随说话人的基音在 60–100 Hz 之间变化。它去掉最低频的成分，也改变了 100–500 Hz 的相位。耳朵听不出这种相位变化，但波形因此对不齐。Speech is encoded with the application set to Voip, so the encoder first passes the input through a high-pass filter whose cutoff frequency moves within 60–100 Hz with the speaker's pitch. The filter removes the lowest frequencies and also changes the phase at 100–500 Hz. The ear cannot hear this phase change, but it keeps the waveforms from lining up.
  • Opus 不编码 20 kHz 以上的声音。合成音乐里的镲片在 20–24 kHz 有能量，解码后这部分就没有了。只比较 20 Hz 到 20 kHz 时，128 kb/s 的音乐从图中的 17.6 dB 升到约 24 dB。Opus does not code sound above 20 kHz. The cymbals in the synthesized music have energy at 20–24 kHz, and that part is gone after decoding. If only frequencies from 20 Hz to 20 kHz are compared, the SNR of the music at 128 kb/s rises from the 17.6 dB shown in the figure to about 24 dB.

码率低时，编码误差才是主要部分。CELT 单独编码每个频带的能量，解码后每个频带的能量接近原来，但频带内部的细节可能和原来差别很大。SILK 用噪声整形让量化噪声的频谱跟着语音频谱走，这样噪声更容易被语音本身掩蔽。评价音质，最终还是要靠听，或者用模拟人耳的客观指标：语音可用 PESQ（Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality，ITU-T P.862）或 POLQA（Perceptual Objective Listening Quality Analysis，ITU-T P.863），音乐可用 ViSQOL（Virtual Speech Quality Objective Listener）的音频模式。Only at low bitrates does coding error make up most of the difference. CELT codes the energy of each band separately, so after decoding the energy of each band is close to the original, but the detail inside a band can differ a lot from the original. SILK uses noise shaping to make the spectrum of the quantization noise follow the spectrum of the speech, so that the speech itself masks the noise more easily. In the end, sound quality has to be judged by listening, or with objective metrics that model human hearing: PESQ (Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality, ITU-T P.862) or POLQA (Perceptual Objective Listening Quality Analysis, ITU-T P.863) for speech, and ViSQOL (Virtual Speech Quality Objective Listener) in its audio mode for music.

把整条通路串起来Putting the whole signal path together

LPC线性预测编码。用前面若干个样本的加权和预测当前样本；权重描述频谱包络。Linear predictive coding. Predicts the current sample as a weighted sum of a number of preceding samples. The weights describe the spectral envelope.
LTP长时预测。用一个基音周期之前的波形预测当前波形。Long-term prediction. Predicts the current waveform from the waveform one pitch period earlier.
NLSF归一化线谱频率。LPC 系数的另一种表示，便于量化和插值。Normalized line spectral frequencies. Another representation of the LPC coefficients that is easier to quantize and interpolate.
激励Excitation预测之后剩下的误差信号，SILK 把它量化成整数脉冲。The error signal left over after prediction. SILK quantizes it into integer pulses.
MDCT改进离散余弦变换。把 2N 个样本变成 N 个系数的实数变换。相邻帧的窗口互相重叠，重叠相加时时域混叠互相抵消。CELT 的窗口只重叠 2.5 毫秒。Modified discrete cosine transform. A real-valued transform that turns 2N samples into N coefficients. The windows of neighboring frames overlap, and their time-domain aliasing cancels out when they are overlap-added. CELT’s windows overlap by only 2.5 ms.
长块 / 短块Long block / short blocksCELT 一帧用一个长变换（长块），遇到瞬态时改用 8 个短变换（短块），避免预回声。CELT codes a frame with one long transform (a long block). For a frame with a transient, it switches to 8 short transforms (short blocks) to avoid pre-echo.
预回声Pre-echo变换编码的量化噪声散到敲击之前原本安静的部分。Quantization noise from transform coding that spreads into the originally quiet stretch before a percussive hit.
频带能量Band energy一个频带内所有系数的平方和开方（实际上是幅度）。CELT 在对数域里先编码它：粗能量 6 dB 一级，精细能量再细分。The square root of the sum of the squares of all coefficients in a band (in effect, an amplitude). CELT codes it first, in the log domain: coarse energy in 6 dB steps, then fine energy subdivides those steps.
PVQ金字塔向量量化。用 K 个整数单位脉冲逼近一个单位向量，码字序号按均匀分布编码。Pyramid vector quantization. Approximates a unit vector with K integer unit pulses. The codeword index is coded with a uniform distribution.
码字Codeword码本里的一个可选值。编码器只传它的序号。One of the possible values in a codebook. The encoder sends only its index.
折叠Folding没有分到比特的频带，复制低频频带已解码的形状来填充。Filling a band that got no bits by copying the already decoded shape of lower-frequency bands.
区间编码Range coding按符号概率不断缩小区间的熵编码方法。An entropy coding method that repeatedly narrows an interval according to the probability of each symbol.
TOC每个 Opus 包的第一个字节，说明模式、带宽、帧长、声道和帧数。Table of contents: the first byte of every Opus packet. It gives the mode, bandwidth, frame duration, channel count, and number of frames.
PLC丢包隐藏。包没收到时，由解码器外推出一段声音。Packet loss concealment. When a packet does not arrive, the decoder extrapolates a stretch of audio to fill the gap.
FEC / LBRR前向纠错（forward error correction）／低码率冗余（low bitrate redundancy）。SILK 在包里附带上一帧的一份低码率副本。上一个包丢了，解码器用它恢复。Forward error correction / low bitrate redundancy. SILK adds a low-bitrate copy of the previous frame to the packet. If the previous packet is lost, the decoder recovers it from this copy.
